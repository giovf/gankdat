# UK Sanctions List (FCDO)

Every designation on the official UK Sanctions List published by the Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office — individuals, entities and ships — with **names and aliases**, designation type, **regime**, designation source, sanctions imposed, countries, date designated and last-updated date. Refreshed daily (Open Government Licence v3).

## What people use it for
- **Sanctions screening and KYC/KYB** — bulk pulls by regime or date for compliance teams and vendors.
- **Monitoring** — designations added or updated since a date.

## Output (one item per designation)
`unique_id, name, aliases, designation_type, regime, designation_source, sanctions_imposed, countries, date_designated, last_updated`

Served as published by the UK government for compliance use; dates of birth, identity numbers, addresses and contact details are dropped. Use for compliance, due-diligence, research or journalism only; verify matches against the official list before acting. Pay per result. Data from gankdat.com.
