# EU Public Tenders (TED)

Contract notices and awards from TED, the official EU procurement journal, flattened to one row per notice: publication number, title, **buyer and country**, notice type, procedure type, contract nature, **CPV codes**, places of performance, **value and currency**, published date, **deadline** and the TED URL. Refreshed daily (Commission Decision 2011/833/EU reuse terms).

## What people use it for
- **Bid intelligence** across EU member states by country, CPV, value or deadline.
- **Competitor and buyer research** from award notices.

## Output (one item per notice)
`publication_number, title, buyer, buyer_country, notice_type, procedure_type, contract_nature, cpv_codes, places_of_performance, value_amount, value_currency, published_at, deadline_at, notice_url`

Organisation-level data only (buyer contact persons are never included). Pay per result; set **Maximum results** to control spend. Data from gankdat.com.
